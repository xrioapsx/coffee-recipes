Method

  1. Stir any honey or syrup into the warm coffee until it dissolves. Cold honey sticks to the shaker.
  2. Add the ice and shake until the shaker is frosty on the outside.
  3. Strain into a glass, over fresh ice if you like.

Closest to a shaken iced drink: the immersion iced coffee. Hoffmann brews strong coffee hot and presses it straight onto ice, so the melting ice does the diluting. A shaker works the same way, which is why the calculator counts ice melt.
